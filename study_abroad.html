ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Understand Your Admission Requirements

Explore eligibility requirements for diploma, bachelor's, master's and doctoral programs.

Diploma Admission Requirements

Academic Requirements

Secondary School Completion Certificate or equivalent.

Language Requirements

IELTS 5.5 or equivalent English proficiency.

Supporting Documents

Passport, transcripts, recommendation letter.

Application Timeline

January and September intakes available.

Bachelor's Admission Requirements

Academic Requirements

High School Certificate with required minimum grades.

Language Requirements

IELTS 6.0–6.5 depending on institution.

Supporting Documents

Passport, transcripts, personal statement.

Application Timeline

Applications typically open 6–12 months before intake.

Master's Admission Requirements

Academic Requirements

Bachelor's Degree in a related field.

Language Requirements

IELTS 6.5–7.0 depending on program.

Supporting Documents

Degree certificate, CV, references and SOP.

Application Timeline

Recommended application 8–12 months before intake.

PhD Admission Requirements

Academic Requirements

Master's Degree with strong academic background.

Language Requirements

IELTS 7.0+ or equivalent.

Supporting Documents

Research proposal, publications and references.

Application Timeline

Rolling admissions or supervisor-led applications.
